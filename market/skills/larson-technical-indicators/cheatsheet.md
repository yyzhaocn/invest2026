# Cheatsheet — Larson (quick reference)

## Golden Rules
- **Parameters decide everything** — tune every indicator.
- **Volume goes with the trend** — confirm with volume.
- **Change of character** = the buy/sell pivot.
- **Confirm before acting** (≥3 days, or 21+15-day PRC, or BOP+MA cross).
- **Use stops** (ATR-based) — most traders' #1 error is no stop.

## Indicator → Parameter → Signal
| Indicator | Parameter | Bullish signal | Bearish signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moving Average | 30-day (short) / 200-day (strong) | price above | price below → set stop |
| 10/20-day cross | 10 & 20 | 10 > 20 (strength/long) | 10 < 20 (short) |
| BOP | 12-day SMA | crosses above zero (accumulation) | crosses below zero (distribution) |
| MACD (+PRC) | 3 EMA's | lines converge/touch, histogram bull | histogram bear change-of-character |
| PRC | 21-day + 15-day | above 50% / 21 turns up | below 50% → sell 100% |
| TSV | leading, vol-based | rising w/ price | falling / diverging |
| RSI | + TSV confirmation | strong above | weak / divergence |
| Inertia | 0-100 | negative (Larson convention) | positive |
| ATR | trailing stop size | — | stop loss trigger |

## Key Numbers
- **1.5×** 20-day avg volume = commitment trigger
- **30**-day MA buy/sell line; **200**-day = strongest level
- **3-day** confirmation window
- **12**-day BOP SMA
- **21 + 15**-day PRC; **50% level** line
- **MACD/PRC** ≈ **90%** success at exact crossover
- **Inertia**: negative = bullish

## Process (12-plus tab / scan)
1. Pick candidates via **stock scans** (≥6 ingredient filters).
2. Open the **same stock across multiple indicator tabs** (MA, volume, BOP, MACD, PRC, TSV, RSI, Inertia, ATR).
3. Tally bullish vs bearish reads → act on majority.
4. Confirm signal(s) per indicator rule.
5. Size/exit with **ATR stop**; re-tune parameters if regime changes; never short a bull market.
