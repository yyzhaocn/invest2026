# Patterns — Reusable Trading Setups (Larson)

Decision rules / thresholds / tells for applying the book's indicators.

## Volume-Confirmation Patterns
- **Healthy uptrend** = price up + volume up (normal, confirms). ⇐ *the trend is your friend.*
- **Upside breakout w/o volume expansion** = suspect; wait for volume to confirm supply/demand favoring demand.
- **Downside breakout + volume expansion** = strongly bearish (more sellers). More volume during downtrend = more sellers.
- **Commitment spike**: daily volume ≥ **1.5× the 20-day average** → real move likely in the current direction.

## Moving-Average Rules (Ch2)
- Price **above 30-day MA** → greater upside potential.
- Price **below 30-day MA** → greater odds of moving lower; **set a stop-loss**.
- **200-day MA** = strongest support/resistance; a break is significant.
- **10-day ↔ 20-day cross**: 10-day breaks down through 20-day → **short**; 10-day moves up through 20-day → **strength/long**.
- **Confirm any MA crossover over ≥3 consecutive days** before acting.
- In a bullish position, if the market may turn: **shorten** your MA (faster sell signal).

## BOP (Ch3)
- **BOP crosses above zero line** → accumulation/bullish change-of-character candidate.
- Wait for **BOP + MA cross of center line** together before entering.
- Use **12-day SMA** on BOP (9-10 too early, 14-15 miss the low).

## MACD (Ch4)
- Look for the **three EMAs coming together closely** (touch/≈converge) — not necessarily crossing.
- If they're too far apart, the setup doesn't work — skip.
- Prefer **histogram** to read change of character: bull→bear = sell, bear→bull = buy.
- **MACD + PRC exact crossover ≈ 90% win rate.**

## PRC (Ch5)
- **21-day PRC** (secret ingredient) + **15-day PRC** oscillator.
- **50% level** = the line: crossing **above 50% = bullish**, **below 50% = bearish**.
- **Sell trigger**: if both 21-day & 15-day PRC drop below 50% → sell **100%** (conservative: ≥50%). Sell ½ at once, ½ once below the level.
- **Buy trigger**: 21-day PRC turns up/above → positive; wait ≥3 consecutive days confirmation.

## Inertia (Ch8)
- **Negative inertia = bullish** uptrend confirmation; **positive inertia = bearish** (Larson convention).
- Price must trend in the same direction as Inertia. Never use Inertia alone.

## ATR / Stops (Ch9)
- **Trailing stop** sized by ATR protects profits and limits losses.
- When price **breaks below 30-day MA**, place stop at a selected price.
- **Option exit**: stop based on what the **underlying stock** does (ATR), NOT on % of option cost.

## Anti-Patterns / Tells
- **Buying a falling "cheap" stock** because "I paid more before" (Intel $100→86→65) — technical weakness keeps it dropping. Use a stop.
- **Default parameters** — the edge dies without tuning.
- **Single bar / single indicator** — always confirm (3-day, 21+15-day PRC, BOP+MA cross).
- A **gap DOWN** is more significant than a gap up for bearish intent.
- **Never short into a bull market.**
