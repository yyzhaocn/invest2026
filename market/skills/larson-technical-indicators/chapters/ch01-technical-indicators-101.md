# Ch1 — Technical Indicators 101

**Theme**: Why indicators beat fundamentals, and how to think about them.

## Core Ideas
- **Technicals > fundamentals**: indicators tell you WHICH stocks to buy/sell and WHEN. Fundamentals pick candidates; technicals time the trade.
- **Parameters are the most essential aspect** of any indicator — right parameters = better entry/exit points.
- **Two styles**: **lagging** (move after price — moving averages) vs **leading** (change before price — TSV, money stream, BOP). The edge is in leading indicators (price+volume based).

## Volume Rules (the foundation)
- **Volume goes with the trend**: normal for volume to rise as price rises, fall as price falls → healthy relationship.
- **Upside breakout must expand volume** to show enthusiasm (supply/demand favors demand).
- **Downside breakout + expanding volume** = strongly bearish (more sellers).
- **Commitment trigger**: daily volume ≥ **1.5× the 20-day average volume** = sign of commitment, raises odds of a move.

## Voice / Style
- "Make the trend your friend." The most successful traders don't care if the market goes up, down, or sideways — they follow what indicators + volume say.
- Market drops "4-8× faster than it goes up" → have a plan (stops) on the downside.

## Self-test anchors
- Leading indicator examples: money stream, money stream/BOP.
- Volume false statement: "normal for volume to increase as prices decline" is FALSE.
- Best answer: use technical indicators; proper parameters = most essential aspect.
