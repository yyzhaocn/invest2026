# Ch10 — Stock Scans with Six Important Indicators

**Theme**: Automate candidate discovery with multi-indicator filters.

## Core Concepts
- **A stock scan = a filter built from indicators + parameters + timeframes** that summarizes a stock's behavior — like your mom's recipe ingredients for a predictable result.
- Build scans for **all market conditions** (bullish and bearish).
- **This is Larson's favorite activity** — combine ≥6 technical ingredients per scan.

## Bullish Scan Example
- Price **above three moving averages** (good sign stock is moving up), plus volume/momentum confirmations (e.g., rising volume above the 20-day average, MACD/PRC turning).
- Rank candidates (e.g., by notes) → analyze the details → the "cream of the crop."

## Bearish Scan Example
- Mirrored criteria: below moving averages + expanding volume on down moves + indicators confirming distribution → short candidates.

## Value
- **Eliminates tedium**: instead of analyzing hundreds of stocks by hand, scans surface strong candidates in any market.
- **Refine over time**: keep tuning parameters as you learn which ingredients best predict behavior.

## Self-test anchors
- Advantage of a screen: save time looking for individual stocks / pinpoint strong candidates. Disadvantage: a huge raw list still needs your analysis.
