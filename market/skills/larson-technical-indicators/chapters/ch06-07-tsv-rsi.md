# Ch6 & Ch7 — Time-Segmented Volume (TSV) & RSI + TSV

**Theme**: Volume as a leading indicator, paired with RSI for confirmation.

## What TSV Is
- **Time-Segmented Volume (TSV)** is a **leading indicator** (Worden/TC 2000) that segments volume over time — because volume anticipates price. It's one of Larson's three starred leading indicators (TSV, money stream, BOP).
- **Leading** = changes *before* price changes; based on **price AND volume**.

## How to Use
- Use TSV **direction** as a leading read on accumulation/distribution.
- **Ch7 pairing**: confirm **RSI + TSV together** — both pointing the same way is a stronger signal than either alone.
- Volume is the enabling force of the trend (ties back to Ch1's "volume goes with the trend").

## Key Numbers / Behavior
- TSV is a *leading* indicator (its key property) — don't treat it as lagging.
- Combined with RSI, use for confirmation rather than standalone signals.

*(The book presents RSI primarily as a confirmation tool combined with TSV rather than as a standalone oscillator.)*
