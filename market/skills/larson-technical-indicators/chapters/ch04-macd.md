# Ch4 — MACD

**Theme**: The convergence/divergence momentum engine — and its 90% partnership with PRC.

## Core Concepts
- **MACD + PRC ≈ 90% success rate**: when Larson can identify the *exact crossing* of these two, he wins ~90% of the time.
- **MACD uses THREE exponential moving averages** (vs one or two for price MAs).
- **Parameters make the biggest difference** — adjust any parameter (0-100); inserting the right number is the key.

## Signals
- **Powerful setup = convergence, not just cross**: the three moving averages come together closely (touch / ~eighth-of-an-inch close). If too far apart, the setup doesn't work well.
- **20/50/200-day touch example**: when fast 20-day breaks through 50 (and 200, strongest resistance), and next day closes lower / gaps down → **bearish** (20 breaking 50 & 200 = strong bearish).
- **Histogram form is preferred** — it makes **change of character** obvious: stock comes straight up = buy; bull→bear change = sell & take profit; repeat.

## Anti-pattern (Intel lesson)
- Buying "because it's cheaper than I paid" (Intel $100→$86→$65) on a technically weak stock → it keeps dropping. **Nobody taught her a stop loss** (see Ch9).

## Self-test anchors
- 90% pair = **MACD and PRC**. Powerful signal = **lines converge**.
- Advantage of histogram = **clearer buy/sell signals** (visually distinct).
- MACD is a **leading** indicator using three exponential MAs.
