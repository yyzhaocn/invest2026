# Ch11 — Three Investment Strategies That Work in Any Market

**Theme**: Market-neutral/regime-adaptive playbook.

## The Three Strategies
1. **Buy stocks that are going UP ("make the trend your friend")** — momentum + volume alignment. For options, buy the call / use trend-aligned variants.
2. **Multi-indicator confirmation** — act only when **multiple indicators give buy signals together** (the 12-plus tab system / ≥3 confirming).
3. **Adapt parameters to the regime** — **change your indicator parameters when the market changes** from bullish to bearish (or vice versa). Don't fight the regime with stale settings.

## Guardrails
- **Never short a bull market** — you can go long in a bear market, but never shorting into strength.
- **Gap down > gap up** significance (a gap down is more significant/more bearish).
- Timeframe flexibility: test over 3 days, 6 months, 1 year to confirm an edge persists.

## Success Mindset
- Success is based on **using the right indicator for the right market** and adapting.
- Armed with these = raise your winning % regardless of market direction; take profits with discipline.

## Self-test anchors
- Multiple indicators giving buy signals is the confirmation trigger.
- Shorting signals are all the opposite of buy signals (yes).
- Can go long in a bear market, never short a bull market (yes).
