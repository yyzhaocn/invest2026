# Ch5 — Price Rate of Change (PRC)

**Theme**: A simple, powerful oscillator — the 90% partner of MACD.

## Core Concepts
- **PRC (Price Rate of Change)** has proven successful ~90% of the time (with MACD).
- **The "secret ingredient" = 21-day parameter**: divide the current close by the close **21 days ago**. Companion line: **15-day PRC** (shorter = faster).

## Signals — the 50% Level
- **Above 50% level = bullish**; **crosses below 50% = bearish**.
- **Sell trigger**: if using 21-day (top) + 15-day (bottom) and PRC drops below → **seriously sell 100%** of the stock (conservative: at least 50%). Ideally **sell ½ right away, ½ when it drops below the level**.
- **Buy trigger**: 21-day PRC turns up / goes above 50% → positive signal (wait ≥3 consecutive days confirmation).

## Confirmation & Timing
- **Wait ~4 trading days** after a signoff for a likely **gap down**.
- Confirm **both the 21-day AND 15-day PRC break below** before selling — that's the double confirmation that usually precedes a gap.
- Smaller parameter (15-day) reacts faster; use 21-day as the primary trend line.

## Note
- PRC reversal/direction IS a buy/short signal in itself; combine 15-day and 21-day oscillators and wait for both to confirm previously-given signals.
