# Ch3 — Balance of Power (BOP)

**Theme**: Institutional accumulation vs distribution behind the tape.

## Core Concepts
- **BOP** tells whether the underlying action is characteristic of **systematic buying (accumulation)** or **distribution**. Plot = accumulation above/below a **zero (center/level) line**. Represents institutional buying/selling in blocks of **≥10,000 shares**.
- More buying → stock up; more selling → stock down; lack of either → **neutral**, often sideways until action intensifies.

## Parameters & Signals
- **Larson's 12-day SMA** on BOP works best: 9/10-day entered too early; 14/15-day missed the low.
- **Bullish setup**: BOP rises above zero line (**change of character**: bearish→neutral→bullish = institutions starting to buy).
- **Entry confirmation**: optionally wait until the **moving average also crosses the zero center line** before entering — then ride the move (e.g., $10 → $14).

## Voice / Style
- BOP is a **leading indicator** (price+volume based) — one of Larson's favorites in TC 2000.
- Conservative approach: wait for both BOP and the MA cross before entering; nothing wrong with confirming.

## Self-test anchors
- BOP indicates institutional buying/selling + volume.
