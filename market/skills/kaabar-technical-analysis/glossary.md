# Glossary

**ALMA (Arnaud Legoux Moving Average)** — Gaussian-windowed MA with offset/shift control for noise filtering (Ch 3)

**Aggressive technique** — default Bollinger/RSI rule: act on band touch / extreme zone directly (Ch 3)

**ATR (Average True Range)** — rolling mean of True Range; the standard volatility unit for stop distances (Ch 6)

**Bollinger bands** — mean ± k·σ envelope; squeeze → breakout watch (Ch 3, 6)

**Break** — in loops, immediately exits the loop (Ch 2)

**CAGR** — compound annual growth rate; annualized return for comparability (Ch 12)

**Confluence** — multiple Fibonacci/S/R levels clustering near one price (Ch 5)

**Conservative technique** — Bollinger/RSI rule awaiting middle-band re-cross or double confirmation (Ch 3)

**Continue** — in loops, skips to the next iteration (Ch 2)

**DCC (Double Conservative Confirmation)** — RSI technique requiring two consecutive conservative confirmations; author's favorite (Ch 3)

**Dataframe** — 2-D tabular pandas structure; iloc/loc access (Ch 2)

**Decorrelation** — designing indicators whose signals are independent of classic tools (Ch 1, 11)

**Doji** — candle with open ≈ close; indecision, reversal watch in context (Ch 7)

**Double bottom/top** — two similar lows/highs + neckline break; bullish/bearish reversal (Ch 10)

**Double trouble pattern** — modern multi-candle pattern not in classic literature (Ch 7)

**Downside deviation** — σ of negative returns only; Sortino input (Ch 12)

**Efficiency ratio (ER)** — |net change| / Σ|per-period changes|; KAMA's trendiness measure (Ch 3)

**Elliott wave theory** — subjective wave-decomposition framework; classic TA's fitting paradox (Ch 1)

**EMH (Efficient Market Hypothesis)** — price = value; TA's "greatest nemesis" (Ch 1)

**Extreme euphoria pattern** — overextended bullish exhaustion configuration (Ch 7)

**Fibonacci timing pattern** — applies Fibonacci ratios to time/duration for turning points (Ch 9)

**Fractal markets** — self-similar patterns across timeframes; basis of harmonic patterns (Ch 8)

**Gartley / Bat / Crab / Butterfly** — XABCD harmonic patterns with distinct D-ratio extremes (Ch 8)

**Golden ratio (ϕ)** — ≈1.618; root of all Fibonacci trading ratios (Ch 5)

**Head and shoulders** — three-peak reversal pattern with neckline (Ch 10)

**Heikin-Ashi** — noise-filtered candles; HA close=(O+H+L+C)/4, HA open=avg(prev HA O,C) (Ch 4)

**Hit ratio** — win rate; must be read with risk-reward (Ch 12)

**HMA (Hull Moving Average)** — WMA(√n) of (2·WMA(n/2) − WMA(n)); low lag + smooth (Ch 3)

**IWMA** — inverse-weighted MA favoring older data; long-term comparison (Ch 3)

**KAMA (Kaufman Adaptive MA)** — volatility-adaptive smoothing via efficiency ratio (Ch 3)

**K's Reversal Indicator I** — MACD-based reversal indicator (Ch 11)

**K's Reversal Indicator II** — price+time+MA 3D signal; author's favorite (Ch 1, 11)

**K's RSI²** — RSI variant using slope-divergence technique (Ch 11)

**LSMA (Least Squares MA)** — regression-line fit over window; trend slope (Ch 3)

**Marginal predictability** — only add uncorrelated, >50%-accuracy indicators (Ch 1)

**Max drawdown** — peak-to-trough decline; capital requirement metric (Ch 12)

**Neckline** — level whose break confirms double-top/bottom or head-and-shoulders (Ch 10)

**Net return** — (end/start − 1)×100; capital-size normalizer (Ch 12)

**OHLC** — open, high, low, close; building blocks of financial time series (Ch 1)

**Pattern** — recurring configuration with empirically expected outcome (Ch 7)

**Profit factor** — gross profits / gross losses (Ch 12)

**Psychological/round levels** — clean numbers acting as natural S/R (Ch 1)

**Regime** — bullish/bearish/ranging market state; weights signal interpretation (Ch 1)

**Resistance** — price ceiling from selling pressure; repeated rejections (Ch 1)

**Risk-reward ratio** — avg win / avg loss; pairs with hit ratio (Ch 12)

**RSI** — relative strength index (Wilder); classic 30/70 oscillator (Ch 1, 3)

**Sharpe ratio** — excess return per unit of total risk; >1 good, >2 very good (Ch 12)

**Slope divergence** — comparing price slope vs indicator slope; reusable technique (Ch 3, 11)

**Sortino ratio** — Sharpe using downside deviation only (Ch 12)

**Support** — price floor from buying pressure; repeated bounces (Ch 1)

**TD setup (DeMark)** — 9-count exhaustion pattern; thrives in ranging markets (Ch 9)

**Time series** — data points measured at successive times; trend/seasonality/cycle/noise (Ch 2)

**True Range** — max(H−L, |H−prevC|, |L−prevC|); handles gaps (Ch 6)

**V technique** — RSI extreme spike then sharp reversal; early signal (Ch 3)

**Volume candlesticks** — candles showing volume context of moves (Ch 4)

**WMA** — recency-weighted moving average (Ch 3)

**WMA/IWMA cross** — one-parameter crossover; WMA=short, IWMA=long (Ch 3)

**Walk-forward testing** — rolling out-of-sample validation (Ch 12)
