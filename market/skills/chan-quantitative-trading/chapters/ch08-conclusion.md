# Chapter 8: Conclusion — Next Steps

## Core Idea
The complete path: find an edge → backtest rigorously → allocate capital (Kelly, fractional) → execute (paper → live) → monitor; alpha decays, so keep the research loop running.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The Full Loop
1. **Fishing for ideas** (Ch2, 6 questions)
2. **Backtesting** (Ch3, costs + survivorship-free data + walk-forward)
3. **Capital allocation & leverage** (Ch6, Kelly/fractional)
4. **Execution** (Ch5, semi → full auto, paper → live)
5. **Monitoring & refinement** (Sharpe vs forward, drawdowns, alpha decay)

### Alpha Decay & Crowding
- Successful strategies get copied → edge shrinks ("alpha decay"); institutions arbitrage crowded ideas.
- The research pipeline is the durable asset, not any single strategy.

## Key Concepts
- **Alpha decay**: edge erosion over time.
- **Research pipeline**: idea → test → deploy → recycle.

## Key Takeaways
1. Treat the process as the business; individual strategies are perishable.
2. Expect and monitor alpha decay; keep a pipeline of candidate ideas.
3. Everything converges on discipline: costs, sizing, survival.
