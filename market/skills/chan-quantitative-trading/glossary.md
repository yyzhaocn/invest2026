# Glossary

**ADF test (Augmented Dickey-Fuller)** — unit-root test for stationarity; confirms a spread is mean-reverting (Ch 7)

**Adjusted close** — price series adjusted for splits and dividends (Ch 3)

**Alpha decay** — edge erosion as strategies get copied/crowded (Ch 8)

**Cointegration** — stationary linear combination of non-stationary series (Ch 7)

**Compound growth rate (g)** — the objective Kelly maximizes (Ch 6)

**Conditional optimization** — regime-dependent parameters (Ch 7)

**Crowding** — institutional adoption arbitraging away an edge (Ch 2, 8)

**Data-snooping bias** — overfitting via too many trials; reported-best backtest overstates live Sharpe (Ch 2, 3)

**Fractional Kelly** — using a fraction of full Kelly to survive estimation error (Ch 6)

**Half-life (of mean reversion)** — how fast a spread reverts; sizes the lookback (Ch 7)

**Information ratio** — annualized Sharpe; Chan's headline metric (Ch 2)

**Kelly formula** — F* = μ/σ² (continuous) or W − (1−W)/R (discrete); optimal leverage (Ch 6)

**Leverage** — portfolio size / equity; Kelly caps it (Ch 6)

**Market impact** — your own order moving the price (Ch 5)

**Mean reversion** — prices revert to mean; needs stationary series (Ch 7)

**Model risk** — parameters wrong even when concept is right; argues for fractional Kelly (Ch 6)

**Momentum** — trends persist; works on non-stationary series (Ch 7)

**Paper trading** — dry-run the full pipeline with simulated fills (Ch 5)

**Reality gap** — backtest vs live Sharpe divergence (Ch 5)

**Scalability** — edge transfers across markets/capital (Ch 1)

**Sharpe ratio** — annualized (excess return)/σ (Ch 3)

**Slippage** — expected vs actual fill price difference (Ch 5)

**Spread** — β-weighted difference used for mean reversion (Ch 7)

**Stationarity** — time-invariant statistical properties (Ch 7)

**Survivorship bias** — database missing delisted/bankrupt names; inflates backtests (Ch 2, 3)

**Transaction costs** — commissions + slippage + impact; the #1 backtest killer (Ch 2, 3, 5)

**Unconditional optimization** — fixed parameters across regimes (Ch 7)
