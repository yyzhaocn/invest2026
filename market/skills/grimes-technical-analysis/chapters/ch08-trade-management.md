# Chapter 8: Trade Management

## Core Idea
Managing a trade (stop, target, scale-out, trail) is as much the edge as the entry — all pre-planned, none emotional.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Placing the Initial Stop
- **Structural**: below the swing low (long) / above swing high (short) — where the setup is invalidated.
- **Volatility-scaled**: stop distance ≈ multiple of recent ATR, so normal noise doesn't kill the trade.

### Setting Price Targets
- Measured moves (range height, pattern projection) and **volatility projection** (past ATR → likely leg length).
- Plan a scale-out ladder (e.g., 1/2 off at first target, trail the rest).

### Active Management
- Scale out into strength; trail stops once profitable; let the runner run.
- **Portfolio considerations**: too many correlated trades = one big bet in disguise; diversify trade types, not just names.

## Key Concepts
- **Initial stop**: invalidation point, set structurally.
- **Scale-out**: partial exits at pre-planned levels.
- **Trailing stop**: locks profit while letting winners run.

## Key Takeaways
1. Stop is set by structure + volatility, never by pain tolerance.
2. Targets pre-planned; scale out; trail the remainder.
3. Watch portfolio-level correlation — the tail risk.
