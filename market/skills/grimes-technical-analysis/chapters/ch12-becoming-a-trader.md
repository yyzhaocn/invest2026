# Chapter 12: Becoming a Trader

## Core Idea
Trading is a craft built from thousands of disciplined reps — screen time, small size, journaling, and process iteration; mastery is the process, not any single trade.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The Development Path
1. **Screen time**: build pattern recognition by watching, not trading.
2. **Small size**: practice execution with survival-sized risk.
3. **Journal everything**: decisions, emotions, violations.
4. **Iterate on process**: fix process flaws, not P&L.

### Mastery
- Mastery = thousands of reps of disciplined execution; the edge compounds with consistency.
- Treat losing as tuition; treat process violations as the real losses.

## Key Takeaways
1. The process is the edge; outcomes are lagging indicators of it.
2. Small size + high reps beats big size + few reps.
3. Every journaled trade is a deposit in your edge account.
