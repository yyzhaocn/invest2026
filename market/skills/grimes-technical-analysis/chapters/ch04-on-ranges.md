# Chapter 4: On Trading Ranges

## Core Idea
Ranges are mean-reverting structures: fade the extremes, exit at mid-range; the range has measurable width and time that define the trade.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Range Dynamics
- Range = bounded oscillation between support and resistance.
- **Range trading**: fade extremes (buy support, sell resistance), exit near middle.
- Range width and duration give the trade's math (reward = half-range, stop = beyond extreme).

### Range Risks
- Ranges break — a "range trade" that becomes a breakout is the classic loss.
- Watch for range contraction (compression) = breakout prelude.

## Key Concepts
- **Range width**: distance between S/R → target math.
- **Compression**: tightening = breakout setup.

## Key Takeaways
1. Fade extremes, exit mid-range; reward/risk comes from range geometry.
2. Never add to a fading position at the extreme.
3. Compression precedes breakouts — be ready to switch to trade #4.
