# Chapter 2: The Market Cycle and the Four Trades

## Core Idea
Markets cycle trend → range → trend; everything tradable falls into four trade types, each with its own rules and failure modes. Know which one you're in.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The Market Cycle
- **Trend → range → trend**, with **transitions** between them — transitions are where most money is lost and made.
- Each phase has distinct structure (persistence vs mean reversion).

### The Four Trades
1. **Trend continuation**: buy pullbacks within an established trend.
2. **Trend termination**: fade the exhaustion/climax at trend end.
3. **Range trading**: fade range extremes (mean reversion).
4. **Range breakout**: trade the transition from range into a new trend.
- Each has different: entry, initial stop, target, and failure mode. Choose deliberately, never by accident.

## Key Concepts
- **Phase**: trend / range / transition.
- **Trade type**: which of the four you're executing.
- **Climax/exhaustion**: trend-termination trigger.

## Key Takeaways
1. Classify the phase first; the trade type follows.
2. Transitions are high-risk, high-reward — trade them only with explicit rules.
3. Never mix trade types (e.g., fading a breakout as a range trade).
