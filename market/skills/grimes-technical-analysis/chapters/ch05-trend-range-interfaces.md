# Chapter 5: Interfaces between Trends and Ranges

## Core Idea
The transition zones (range→trend, trend→range) are where accounts are made and broken; they demand explicit rules, not improvisation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The Breakout Trade (range → trend)
- **Range breakout**: entry on the transition with confirmation (not just a poke above the range).
- Stop inside the range (structural); target = range height projected.
- Failure = the "failure test" — breakout that fails and returns to range (trade the reversion).

### Trend Termination (trend → range)
- Exhaustion/climax → the range begins; trade the fade or stand aside.
- Parabolic climaxes: the anti-trend setup — extreme vertical moves revert hard.

## Key Concepts
- **Failure test**: breakout fails → strong mean-reversion signal.
- **Parabolic climax**: vertical exhaustion → reversal.

## Key Takeaways
1. Trade transitions with confirmation, never on the poke alone.
2. Failed breakouts are among the best fade setups.
3. At climaxes, the asymmetry flips — stand aside or fade with tight rules.
