# Glossary — Trading Price Action: Trends

**ABC correction** — A two-legged pullback in a trend (A down, B small with-trend, C down deeper); the C-leg breakout is the entry (High 2 / Low 2) (Intro, Ch 1, Ch 20)

**Always-in** — The position you'd hold if forced to be in the market at all times; the core directional read (Intro, Ch 6, Ch 21, Ch 23)

**Barbwire** — A very tight trading range; avoid ioi breakouts there (Ch 7)

**Bar counting** — Counting pullback legs: first bar above the prior bar's high = High 1, next = High 2, etc.; mirror for Low 1/2 (Intro, Ch 1)

**Breakout** — Transition from two-sided (range) to one-sided (trend) behavior (Ch 3)

**Breakout gap** — A strong trend bar at the start of a trend; all breakouts are functionally gaps (Intro, Ch 2, Ch 21)

**Breakout pullback** — A failed breakout whose failure reverses into the opposite setup; the second attempt (Ch 13, Ch 16, Ch 17, Ch 22)

**Breakout test** — A pullback that tests the breakout point; a sign of strength if it doesn't overlap it (Ch 18, Ch 19)

**Buy climax / sell climax** — Market went too far too fast in one direction; ends at the first pause bar; requires a breakout to become a reversal (Ch 2, Ch 6)

**Buying pressure** — Cumulative institutional buying; signs: tails at bar bottoms, bull reversal bars at downswing bottoms, growing large bull bodies (Ch 2)

**Channel** — Price confined between a trend line and a trend channel line (or horizontal lines); a sloping trading range (Ch 14, Ch 15)

**Climactic reversal** — A climax (spike) followed by a strong breakout bar the other way; every reversal is one on some time frame (Ch 2)

**Countertrend** — A trade/setup against the current always-in direction; low probability, needs strong signals (Ch 4, Ch 5)

**Doji** — Bar with tiny/nonexistent body; a one-bar trading range; "close is close enough" (Ch 2, Ch 4)

**Double bottom / double top** — Two pushes testing a prior extreme; second push failing = reversal/pullback likely (Ch 20, Ch 5)

**Entry bar** — The bar during which your order fills (Ch 4)

**Failed breakout** — A breakout that fails to follow through and reverses; the recurring theme — most breakouts fail (Ch 3, Ch 13)

**Follow-through** — The bar(s) after the signal bar in your direction; confirms the move (Ch 2, Ch 4)

**Gap test** — The pullback after a spike, testing the spike (= gap) (Ch 21)

**High 1 / High 2 / High 3** — Pullback leg counts in a bull trend/range; High 2 = ABC; High 3 = wedge bull flag (Intro, Ch 1, Ch 15)

**Inside bar** — Bar contained within the prior bar's range; reversal/continuation signal if small (Ch 6)

**ii / iii** — Two/three consecutive inside bars (Ch 6)

**ioi** — Inside-outside-inside pattern; breakout mode (Ch 6, Ch 7)

**Leg** — A change in the direction of movement; every larger leg is made of smaller legs (Ch 20)

**Low 1 / Low 2 / Low 3** — Rally leg counts in a bear trend/range (Intro, Ch 1)

**Measuring gap** — A pullback from a breakout that doesn't overlap the breakout point (Intro, Ch 19, Ch 21)

**Micro channel** — Extremely tight channel with no/minimal pullbacks; a spike on HTF (Ch 16)

**Micro double bottom / top** — Consecutive bars with identical lows/highs; reversal patterns (Ch 6, Ch 10)

**Micro gap** — Strong trend bar with a gap between the prior and next bar; a breakout test and sign of strength (Intro, Ch 19)

**Micro trend line** — 2–10 bar trend line where most bars touch; used for with-trend setups only (Ch 16)

**Outside bar** — High above prior high AND low below prior low; a one-bar trading range (Ch 6, Ch 7)

**Pullback** — Transition from trend to temporary trading range; the entry opportunity (Ch 3)

**Reversal bar** — Bar reversing some aspect of the prior bar(s); bull: close above open or midpoint (Ch 5)

**Reversal day** — Day that trends one direction then the opposite into the close (Ch 24)

**Second entry** — The market's second attempt to reverse/break out; almost always better than the first (Ch 10)

**Setup** — Chart pattern suggesting an order placement; becomes a signal bar only when a trade fills (Ch 4)

**Signal bar** — Prior bar once your entry fills; labeled in hindsight (Ch 4)

**Spike** — Strong trend bar(s) covering many points with little/no pullback; = gap = climax = breakout (Ch 2, Ch 21)

**Spike and channel trend** — The master pattern: spike → pullback → channel → measured move (Ch 21)

**Stairs** — Trending trading range variant with 3+ ranges; shrinking stairs = waning momentum (Ch 26)

**Test** — Return to an area of support/resistance; behavior at the test decides the trade (Ch 3)

**Trading range** — Largely sideways price action; two-sided control (Ch 1, Ch 3)

**Trend bar** — Bar whose close trended away from the open; body = trending strength (Ch 2)

**Trend from the open** — Day forming one extreme in the first few bars and trending all day (Ch 23)

**Trend line** — Line across the lows of a bull trend (below) or highs of a bear trend (above) (Ch 13)

**Trend resumption day** — Strong first-hour trend, midday range, trend resumes into the close (Ch 25)

**Trending trading range day** — Trend made of 2+ trading ranges separated by breakouts (Ch 22)

**Two legs / two-legged** — The market tries everything twice; moves subdivide into two legs (Ch 20)

**Vacuum effect** — Traders hold back expecting a level to be touched → imbalance → sharp move → fade at the level (Ch 2, Ch 15)

**Wedge** — Converging trend line + trend channel line; reversal setup (Ch 14, Ch 15)

**With-trend** — A trade/setup in the direction of the current always-in trend (Ch 4)
